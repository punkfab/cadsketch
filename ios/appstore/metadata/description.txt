CADSketch turns freehand drawing into precise 2D geometry — and then into 3D parts — right on your iPad.

Sketch with your finger or Apple Pencil. CADSketch recognizes lines, arcs, and circles and cleans them up with a constraint solver, so rough strokes become exact, well-proportioned geometry. Close a profile and it extrudes into a solid you can orbit in 3D.

• Draw and auto-constrain — strokes snap to clean lines, arcs, and circles
• Extrude closed profiles into 3D solids
• Sketch on a face to add material or cut into a part, with the direction and result shown clearly
• Turn text into sketches and emboss it onto a surface
• Build multi-part assemblies
• Drag points to edit; the geometry re-solves live

No account, no sign-in, and your work stays on your device.
